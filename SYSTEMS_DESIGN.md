# EPECUÉN: NO ESCAPE — Systems & Lore Design Bible

## 1. Setting & Atmosphere (Historical Grounding)
- **Location**: Villa Epecuén, Adolfo Alsina, Buenos Aires Province, Argentina.
- **Background**: Once a glamorous thermal spa resort on the shores of Lake Epecuén, renowned for waters with salinity 10x higher than the Atlantic Ocean.
- **The Tragedy**: On November 10, 1985, after an unprecedented rainy cycle, the earth dyke gave way. Brine swept through the streets. The town remained drowned under 10 meters of toxic salt water for nearly 25 years.
- **The Present**: The receding waters revealed a skeletal, calcified necropolis: petrified white eucalyptus trees, broken hotel facades, rusted slaughterhouse towers, and thick salt crusts that crackle underfoot.

## 2. Core Horror Pillars
1. **Auditory Paranoia & Surface Resonance**:
   - Walking on dry salt crust crunches loudly, projecting acoustic signatures that monsters can detect.
   - Players must crouch-walk (`LeftControl` / `C`) on salt crust or risk alerting entities.
2. **Hypersaline Water Hazard**:
   - The water is corrosive mineral brine. Prolonged wading causes caustic burns and hypothermia.
   - Splashing through water produces large audio waves.
3. **Psychological Deterioration (Sanity)**:
   - Prolonged darkness, exposure to brine, and monster proximity trigger hallucinations.
   - Auditory: phantom church bells ringing underwater, mournful Spanish whispers, phantom footsteps mimicking the player.
   - Visual: camera pulsation, peripheral shadow figures, sudden head twitches.
4. **Diegetic Resources**:
   - Heavy metal incandescent flashlight. Consumes battery quickly.
   - Flickers when near the entity or when low on power.
   - Rusted keys, fuses for hotel generators, and evacuation diary notes.

## 3. Entity Design: "El Vigilante de la Sal"
- **Nature**: A calcified entity coated in white salt crystals, moving with unnatural stiffness through flooded arches.
- **Behaviors**:
  - **Dormant / Stalking**: Patrols through dead trees and rubble.
  - **Sound Investigation**: Reacts to footsteps, running on salt, and flashlight clicks.
  - **Charge**: Fast sprint when line of sight is established.
  - **Submerge**: Can vanish into deep murky brine pools and emerge near players.
